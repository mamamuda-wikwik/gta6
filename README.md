An open-world crime game in a sun-soaked coastal city of its own: Neon Bay. Rico owes the fixer Mara Quinn, and four jobs stand between him and a clean slate. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars, dodge the police, and mind the wanted stars.

## The game

- **A city to explore** — a road grid with buildings, palm trees, a park, a beach and an ocean, bigger than one screen, with a camera that follows you.
- **On foot and by car** — walk with WASD or the arrow keys, press E to get in (or out of) a car, drive with W/S to accelerate and brake and A/D to steer.
- **Traffic and pedestrians** — cars cruise the roads and people walk the sidewalks; hit one and the city notices.
- **Wanted level** — steal a car or punch a pedestrian and the stars rise; police cars chase you, ram your car, and bust you on foot. Lose them, wait it out, or pay the spray shop to scrub the heat.
- **Four missions and a story** — Dock Pickup, Hot Wheels, Beach Run (timed), and The Getaway, each with a marker, a compass arrow and a distance readout.
- **A HUD with a minimap** — cash, wanted stars, health and car health, the mission objective, and a live minimap of the city, the police and the marker.
- **Extras** — cash bags hidden around the city, synthesized sound effects and a police siren (M to mute), and a best score saved in the browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.

Or open `game/index.html` directly from this repo — the game runs fully offline and loads nothing over the network.
